Always in an arch
Every picture sits in the same temple-arch frame — round on top, square at the bottom. It is the one shape doing all the cultural work, so nothing else has to.
Photos replace them, one by one
A scraped listing starts with its category picture. When a real photo arrives — from the owner, or from you visiting — it takes the same arch and the illustration steps aside.
Never a stock photo
A generic curry photo on a listing is worse than a drawing: it implies you've been there. The illustration is honest about what the app knows.
